For some background, my name is collin kanofsky and I am a senior in highschool with 7 years of FIRST robotics experience and just finished my senior project. I built a robot I call the Robopack which is a backpack carrying robot that follows a human on its own using a camera. The Robopack currently uses a Raspberry Pi 5 with an Raspberry Pi AI Camera, as well as a usb connection to a Seeed RP2040 Xiao which controls the 2 CIM motors attached to motor drivers that are controlled via PWM.
