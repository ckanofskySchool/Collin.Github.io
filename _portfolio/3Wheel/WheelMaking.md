## Molded Wheel Process (From Daily Journal)

I designed a 4-part 3D printed mold with a hex hub insert so the wheel can mount directly to the drive shaft. The mold is clamped together with a printed top plate and a bottom cover to keep pressure even. For the rubber, I used ReoFlex 30A, mixed 1:1 by volume.

### First Mold (1/16/2026)

The first pour had a leak between the hub and the bottom plate. I improvised a weighted press using a toolbox to seal it and finished the pour just in time as the rubber thickened.

### Process Improvements (1/21/2026)

I printed a top clamp with a funnel so I could pour faster without overflow. This cut the mold time from ~90 minutes to ~20 minutes.
